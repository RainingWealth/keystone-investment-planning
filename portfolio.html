How this portfolio is built

  • Growth engine — Global equities do the compounding; diversified across markets so no single region carries the load.
  • Shock absorber — Bonds and fixed income smooth out the ride when markets dip, so the portfolio doesn't force a panic decision.
  • Inflation hedge — Real assets help the portfolio keep pace when prices rise.
  • Dry powder — A small cash buffer means you never have to sell at the wrong time.
